### The ground under your boots matters now.

Paved road, stone floor, plank deck, meadow grass, swamp mud, deep snow — every step reads what you stand on, and that sets how fast you sprint and walk. Lay a road and it pays you back every time you run it.

A rune-icon names the ground under you, and every footstep strikes a spark of runes in its colour. The server holds the numbers, so everyone runs by the same law.

<details>
<summary>📖 <b>Read the saga</b> — why a road is not a floor, and how the land is read</summary>

**The road is not the floor.** A paved road is the land itself, worked with the hoe. A stone floor is a building you placed on top of it. ThunderFeet reads them in different ways, so they can never be mixed up, and each has its own speed: make the road the highway and the hall floor a stroll.

**What you paint wins.** A hoe path through the swamp is a dirt path, not swamp mud. A paved road up a mountain is a road, not snow. Farmland you cultivated is farmland wherever it lies.

**Steep ground is rock.** Unpainted slopes past the angle where Valheim starts drawing rock count as rock, whatever the biome.

**Thirty-one grounds in all** — every biome's floor, every building material, ship decks, boulders, logs, dungeon floors, shallow water and tar — and each one has its own sprint speed and its own walk speed.

</details>

<details>
<summary>📖 <b>Read the saga</b> — who sees them, and what they cost</summary>

**Everyone sees everyone's runes.** They ride on the footstep every player's game already receives, so they cost the network nothing — and you see them on the steps of players who do not even run ThunderFeet.

**Standing still costs nothing.** The runes only exist while someone walks near you, and fade within a second. No runes rise in water or tar.

**Every rune is drawn by the mod** — sixteen Elder Futhark glyphs, glowing. Turn them down to only your own, or off, or change how many rise; the server can switch them off for everyone.

</details>

{{rune ᚠ | THE TOOLBOX | QUICK START · HOW-TO | 1b1b1b | 8b1e1e | e8c35a}}

**First ten minutes:** install on the server and every player → step from grass onto a paved road and watch the icon change → sprint, and look behind you → open Configuration Manager and tune a ground.

<details>
<summary>🛠️ <b>Make the roads worth building</b></summary>

Raise **Paved road** {{key Sprint Speed %}} to 130 and drop **Swamp mud** to 70. Players will start paving the routes they run.

</details>

<details>
<summary>🛠️ <b>Turn the runes down</b></summary>

Set {{key Rune Footsteps}} to `OnlyMine` to see only your own, or `Off`. {{key Rune Intensity}} sets how many rise and how big (0.25 to 3). A server owner can switch them off for everyone with {{key Rune Footsteps Allowed}}.

</details>

<details>
<summary>🛠️ <b>Show the icon only when it matters</b></summary>

Set {{key Show Surface Icon}} to `OnlyWhenSpeedChanges`: the icon hides on ground that leaves you at vanilla speed.

</details>

<details>
<summary>🛠️ <b>The edges of my road do not count</b></summary>

Road paint fades at its edges. Lower {{key Paint Threshold}} (0.5 by default) to count more of the faded edge as road.

</details>

<details>
<summary>🛠️ <b>Which ground am I on?</b></summary>

Turn on {{key Log Surface Changes}} and every change of ground is written to the BepInEx log with where it happened.

</details>
